Lydon Farrell Property are delighted to present 15 Chestnut Hall, a two bedroom apartment to the market, This own door apartment is located on the ground floor and is presented in very good condition throughout. The apartment is positioned in a securely gated development, within walking distance of all local amenities that Johnstown has to offer including Mace Convenience Store, O'Brien's Restaurant and Bar, Staunton's Pharmacy, Johnstown Shopping centre and public transport which links Navan to the city centre.

This spacious two bedroom apartment benefits from own door access and comprises of an open plan kitchen / living area, a double bedroom, a master ensuite bedroom and main bathroom.

The apartment benefits from a designated parking space and ample communal visitor spaces accessed via electronic gates. Johnstown is easily accessible by local bus and provides direct access onto the N3 and M3. Navan town centre is just a short drive from the apartment.
